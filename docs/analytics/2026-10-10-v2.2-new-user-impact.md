# New-user behavior after Shogo 2.2

Measured against the production platform database on 2026-10-10, about
18:50 UTC. v2.2.0 was tagged
2026-10-05 05:45 UTC. The release moved new users into a workspace chat
with their agent, instead of starting them in a project.

**Rerun on about 2026-10-15.** The post-2.2 day 1–3 return rate below is
based on 53 people. The rest of that cohort was not three days old yet.

## Headline

New users are not signing up faster, and they are not paying. The ones who
do sign up are more likely to send a message, and they send a few more of
them, almost all inside the workspace chat rather than a project. Creating
a project they can see has fallen. Nothing here says the agent is failing
to answer: about 95% of first human messages still get a reply.

| Cohort (signup time, UTC) | Signups | Sent a human message in 24h | Human messages / signup in 24h | Visible project in 24h | Came back and messaged on day 1–3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sep 14–20 | 313 | 60% | 3.7 | 61% | 7.7% (313 eligible) |
| Sep 21–27 | 197 | 73% | 4.1 | 53% | 9.1% (197) |
| Sep 28 – Oct 5 05:45 | 156 | 72% | 4.7 | 30% | 7.7% (156) |
| Oct 5 05:45 – Oct 10 (2.2) | 123 | 81% | 4.9 | 37% | 17% (53 eligible) |

Signup pace was already down before the release: about 45 real signups a
day in the week of Sep 14, about 28 a day in the week of Sep 21, and about
22 a day from Sep 28 through Oct 10. 2.2 did not reverse that, and it did
not make it worse.

"Human message" excludes prompts the workspace agent writes into a project
via `project_call`. Those started landing on Oct 7. In the post-2.2 cohort
they add 0.47 messages per signup on top of the 4.9, across 14 of the 123
people. Counting them as the user's own messages would overstate engagement.
94 of the 150 project chats that received a user-role message in the week
of Oct 5 were opened by `project_call`, not by a person.

## What changed in the product, and what the data shows

Workspace chat became the default place a new user talks, starting the week
of Sep 23 (destination-first onboarding), not on Oct 5. Share of new users'
messages in the first 72 hours:

| Signup window | People | Messaged a project chat | Messaged a workspace chat | Project-chat messages | Workspace-chat messages |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Sep 12–18 | 306 | 193 | 1 | 1,493 | 7 |
| Sep 19–25 | 255 | 107 | 49 | 924 | 291 |
| Sep 26 – Oct 4 | 202 | 8 | 145 | 200 | 811 |
| Oct 5–10 | 131 | 21 | 104 | 88 | 746 |

The Oct 5–10 project-chat count still includes delegated `project_call`
prompts. The human-only table above is the one to use for "did the person
talk."

Visible projects fell because new workspaces now get a hidden anchor
project. Within 72 hours of signup, the share of people who created any
project (including hidden) only slipped from 65% (Sep 12–18) to 55%
(Oct 5–10). Within 24 hours, a *visible* project went from 61% to 37%, and
a hidden one from 0% to 18%. People are not failing to get a workspace.
They are less often making a second, visible project on day one.

Team chat (channels and DMs, `conversation_messages`) is new with 2.2 and
barely used. The first human team-chat messages in production were on
Oct 5. From Oct 5 through Oct 9 the whole platform saw 74 of them, from a
handful of authors per day. Four people in the post-2.2 signup cohort sent
one. Huddles are not in this count.

Onboarding completion stayed high (roughly 90–100% each day). Median time
from signup to first message was 2.5 minutes in mid-September, 1.0 minute
in the Sep 28 week (workspace chat is the first screen), and 2.8 minutes
after 2.2. First session is split between web and mobile; desktop is about
1% of new accounts.

Paid conversion is a separate problem, and it started before 2.2. See below.

The last completed Checkout was 2026-10-01 22:14 UTC (Pro, monthly, $20).
The matching `subscriptions` row exists. Every session since then is
`unpaid` and has no subscription. Amounts are the normal prices (Basic
$8, Pro $20, Business $40). A few sessions on Oct 8 created a Stripe
customer and still expired unpaid, which means the person reached hosted
Checkout and did not pay. Three sessions at 11:00 UTC on Oct 7 were Basic
monthly, Basic annual, and Pro annual a minute apart: someone opening
plans and leaving.

`#1170` (Oct 3) did not change Checkout creation. It updated a staging
test so it can open Stripe's newer "Card" accordion. Session creation
still works; the sessions above were created after that commit.

Fewer people are also *starting* Checkout (about 50 sessions in the week
of Aug 10, 12 in the week of Oct 5). The wide home composer still sends
"Upgrade" to `/billing`, and the sidebar still shows "Upgrade to Pro" for
unpaid workspaces. The phone home composer does not: `usesMobileWorkspaceChrome`
renders `ChatInput` without `onUpgradeClick`
([apps/mobile/app/(app)/index.tsx](../../apps/mobile/app/(app)/index.tsx)).
The phone bottom nav has no billing tab. Checkout is still reachable from
Settings, the project top bar, and the sidebar drawer, which is why
sessions keep getting created.

## How this was measured

Read-only queries against the production database. Prisma columns are
camelCase and must be quoted. Connection details stay out of this repo.

Real signups exclude `@shogo.ai`, and any email matching `e2e`, `test`, or
`example.com`.

Cohorts are cut at `2026-09-21`, `2026-09-28`, and `2026-10-05 05:45:00`
(the v2.2.0 tag). A human message is a `chat_messages` row with
`role = 'user'` whose session is not a `project_call` chat. Until those
rows are stamped (see below), a delegated prompt is one whose session
`inferredName` is `"<caller>: <message>"` and contains the first 25
characters of that session's first user message. A visible project is a
non-hidden `projects` row in a workspace the user belongs to, created
within 24 hours of signup. Day 1–3 return is a human message between 24
and 72 hours after signup, and the denominator is only signups older than
3 days.

`signup_attributions` cannot explain where these users came from. The table
has 14 rows, all from March 2026. The client captured UTMs and called
`POST /api/users/me/attribution`, but email signups fire that call before a
session exists when verification is required (the error is swallowed), and
Google sign-in never called it. Acquisition source for this window is unknown.

## Follow-ups recorded with this measurement

- Delegated `project_call` prompts were stored as `role = 'user'` and
  `agent = 'technical'`, so funnels that filter on that pair counted them
  as a person. New rows use `agent = 'project_call'`. Analytics queries
  already require `agent = 'technical'`, so they skip the new rows. The
  technical chat list still returns them, so the called project's transcript
  still shows the prompt. Rows written before the change are unchanged;
  the name heuristic above is how this report excluded them.
- Signup attribution is now queued until the account exists and the user
  is signed in, including after email verification and after the Google
  round trip. Returning accounts (older than 10 minutes for Google/Apple,
  7 days for email) do not get a row. Historical signups stay unattributed.
