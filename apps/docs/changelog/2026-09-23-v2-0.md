Shogo 2.0 introduces personal workspaces, a free space where your own AI companion works alongside you, and Hoshi 2.0, our new default model. Business and Enterprise plans now include unlimited workspaces for better organization. It also rolls up everything shipped since 1.12: a redesigned workspace, scheduled routines, new models, and a long list of reliability fixes.

<!-- truncate -->

## Personal workspaces

- **A free personal space for everyone.** Every account now includes a personal workspace alongside a team workspace, so you get two free workspaces: one just for you and one to share.
- **Unlimited workspaces for Business and Enterprise.** Create as many workspaces as you need to organize teams, clients, or projects. They share usage, billing, and seats with the parent workspace.
- **Your own AI companion.** Your personal space comes with a companion you can name and give an avatar and personality. It runs on Hoshi 2.0 by default.
- **Goals with approvals.** Give your companion a goal and follow along as it plans, reports progress, and asks for your approval before important steps. Results show up as artifact cards you can open directly.
- **Scheduled routines.** Ask your companion to do something on a schedule, for example "every weekday at 9am, summarize my inbox", and it runs automatically in your timezone. Ask your companion to change, pause, or remove a routine at any time.
- **Side chats and notes.** Start side conversations without losing your main thread, and keep persistent notes your companion can work with.
- **See what's happening now.** The Activity page shows chats and agent tasks that are running right now, in both personal and team workspaces.

## Hoshi 2.0

- **A new default model.** Hoshi 2.0 now powers your personal companion and Shogo's built-in assistant by default. It reasons before it acts, working through a problem before it answers.
- **Same price.** Hoshi 2.0 costs the same as Hoshi 1.0.
- **Available on the API.** Use `hoshi-2-0` with the OpenAI-compatible public API. Hoshi 1.0 (`hoshi-1-0`) stays available as a legacy model while you migrate.

## New

- **Destination-first onboarding.** New accounts start by choosing whether they're setting up for themselves or a team, can invite teammates during setup, and get a short checklist to get going.
- **Redesigned workspace navigation.** A unified chat shell, workspace tabs that match across web and desktop, a project sidebar you can open from chat, and a native "liquid glass" look on iOS.
- **Chat and plan history search.** Find past conversations and plans quickly.
- **Universal chat dock.** Queued messages, running tasks, and checklists live in one dock above the composer, and each turn's agent work collapses into a tidy "Worked for X" summary.
- **Survives connection drops.** If your internet drops mid-turn, chats pause and pick up where they left off, and messages you send while offline are queued and delivered when you're back.
- **Audio input.** Models that understand audio can now take voice and audio input directly.
- **Docker Compose projects.** Run multi-service projects on Shogo with exposed ports, tunnels, and public previews.
- **Shogo in Slack.** Install the Slack app and talk to one Shogo agent for your whole workspace. It pulls in the right project as the conversation needs it.
- **Custom subagents.** Drop agent definitions into `.shogo/agents/*.md` and they load automatically.
- **New models.** Claude Opus 5, Sonnet 5, and Fable 5.1, plus GPT-6 Astra and the GPT-5.6 family (Sol, Terra, Luna). Image generation now defaults to gpt-image-2.5-flare.
- **Desktop beta channel.** Opt in under Settings → Updates to get new desktop builds as soon as they land on main.
- **Publishing upgrades.** Push your dev database to the live published app, keep static apps always-on, and show a friendly loading page while a sleeping app wakes up.
- **New templates.** Restaurant website and storefront templates, plus support for your own custom routes.
- **Appearance settings.** Customize how Shogo looks, with a redesigned process panel.
- **What's New.** You'll now see a short summary like this one in the app after each major release.

## Improved

- **Context usage breakdown.** Click the context meter in chat to see what's using your context window.
- **Model picker.** Hover a model to see more detail before you pick it.
- **Mobile polish.** A smoother project drawer, better keyboard handling in search, cleaner activity and marketplace screens, and a unified color palette across mobile and web.
- **Canvas builds.** Canvases are type-checked before they're shown, and build errors appear in the build log instead of failing silently.

## Fixed

- **Chat reliability.** Fixed slowdowns while responses stream in, and assistant messages not saving in workspace chats without a project.
- **Images.** Retired DALL-E models were removed, and images from your personal companion now display correctly.
- **Windows desktop.** Hardened chat, process handling, and cloud recovery on Windows, and fixed path handling for beta updates.
- **Live models.** Desktops connected to the cloud now see Live models correctly.
